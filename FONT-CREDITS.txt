Permit and serial numbers use matching regular-weight system monospace type (Consolas / Courier New). Pale loop engraving is original decorative SVG artwork. No official FE-Schrift or E-13B font is embedded.
Visual lettering is not a cryptographic authenticity check. Ed25519 signatures are the separate optional verification mechanism.
