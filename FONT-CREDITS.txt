Secure Number-style numerals: original SVG artwork inspired by FE-Schrift design principles. Not an official FE-Schrift font. No E-13B glyphs are used.
Visual lettering is not a cryptographic authenticity check. Ed25519 signatures are the separate optional verification mechanism.
